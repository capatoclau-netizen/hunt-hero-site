How it works

The game loop

  1. Buy $HUNT on pump.fun, connect your wallet in the game (MetaMask, Phantom, Solflare or Backpack) and deposit. Your wallet only asks you to approve the transfer.
  2. Buy heroes for 25,000 $HUNT each. Each one rolls a rarity, stats and perks.
  3. Send up to 15 heroes to hunt. They walk the dungeon and break wood, iron, gold and crystal blocks. Each block pays a fixed reward from the reward pool.
  4. Heroes spend energy on every hit and rest to recover it. Training dummies make rest faster.
  5. Claim your in-game balance to your wallet once it reaches 100,000 $HUNT.
Rewards only accrue while you are online. Close the game and hunting stops. Energy keeps recovering for up to 12 hours while you are away, and rested heroes go back to work when you return. This keeps rewards going to people who actually play.

Reward pool

Rewards come only from real spending

The pool started at zero. It grows only when players buy heroes, items and dummies, pay fees, or trade on the market. Hunting rewards are paid out of it at fixed rates per block:

BlockHPReward ($HUNT)
Wood8035.625
Iron17081.25

Prisoner cage2,000Frees a hero (40%) or an item (60%)
If the pool runs dry, rewards pause. The game shows POOL DRY and hunting rewards stop until new spending refills it. The pool never borrows from deposits or prints tokens. Nobody can promise a return; your rewards depend on how much the community spends.
Transparency

Deposits sit in the public Hunt Hero Treasury

Every deposit goes to one on-chain wallet, the Hunt Hero Treasury. Anyone can check its balance at any time on Solscan:

Hunt Hero TreasuryGh1b9qDhuHmRyASrFnukCF2haQKhGDYbkLEMx1WQ6iPK View on Solscan
  • The server checks every 10 minutes that the vault holds at least what all players are owed. If it ever does not, claims pause automatically until it does.
  • Deposits are credited only after the Solana transaction is finalized.
  • Burned $HUNT is collected and destroyed on-chain in batches. Each batch is a public burn transaction that lowers the total supply.

Staking

Lock $HUNT for hero buffs

Stake from 500,000 $HUNT for 7, 15 or 30 days. Larger stakes and longer locks give bigger stat buffs to your Super Rare and better heroes. Leaving early costs a penalty (50% at 7 days, 30% at 15, 15% at 30), split half to the pool and half to the burn.

Market and invites

Trade and grow together

  • Player market: list heroes and items for $HUNT. The 15% fee is split half to the pool and half to the burn. Minimum prices per rarity stop dumping.
  • Invite link: every player gets a personal link. For every 15 heroes bought by the players you invite, you get 1 free hero.
  • Launch promo: the first 10 wallets that buy a hero get a second one free.

Read before you play

Risks

Hunt Hero is a game, not an investment. $HUNT is a volatile token and can lose most or all of its value. Rewards are paid from a pool funded by player spending and can pause when the pool is empty. There are no guaranteed returns. The game is in active development, and fees and balance numbers may be tuned to keep the economy healthy. Only play with money you can afford to lose.
